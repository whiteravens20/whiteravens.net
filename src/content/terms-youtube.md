These terms cover **White Ravens Publisher**, the tool White Ravens uses to upload its own videos to its YouTube channel, [youtube.com/@whiteravens20](https://www.youtube.com/@whiteravens20). They add to our general [terms of use](/terms/).

## 1. What the tool does

White Ravens Publisher uploads White Ravens' videos to its YouTube channel, always as private, and follows each video until a person makes it public in YouTube Studio. It uses **YouTube API Services**.

## 2. Who may use it

Only White Ravens, for its own channel. The tool is not offered to the public; it has no sign-up and no public interface.
